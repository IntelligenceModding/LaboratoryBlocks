## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Artemis' Laboratory Blocks a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to act respectfully, constructively, and in good faith.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful of different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing criticism on the work rather than the person;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- helping keep discussions relevant and productive;
- being patient with contributors of different experience levels.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory, hateful, or deliberately offensive language;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- repeatedly provoking arguments or derailing project discussions;
- publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission;
- other behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in a collaborative project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to spaces directly related to Artemis' Laboratory Blocks, including:

- GitHub issues;
- pull requests and reviews;
- commit discussions;
- project-related discussions;
- other official spaces where someone is participating on behalf of the project.

Discussion and criticism of code, features, design decisions, and project direction are welcome.

Disagreement itself is not a violation of this Code of Conduct as long as it remains respectful and constructive.

## Enforcement

The project owner and authorized Intelligence Modding team members are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these standards.

They may edit, hide, lock, reject, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, commits, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior, actions may include:

1. a reminder or request to change the behavior;
2. a formal warning;
3. temporary restrictions from participating in project spaces;
4. a permanent ban from participating in the project.

Serious violations may result in stronger action without requiring each previous step.

Moderation decisions will be made as fairly and consistently as reasonably possible.

## Reporting

Abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to:

**report AT intelligence-modding DOT de**

Reports may be reviewed by the project owner or authorized Intelligence Modding team members.

Please include enough information to understand the situation, such as relevant links, screenshots, or descriptions where appropriate.

Reports will be handled privately, and reasonable efforts will be made to protect the privacy and security of the person making the report.

Normal disagreements about project direction, rejected feature requests, declined pull requests, or technical decisions should not be reported as Code of Conduct violations unless they involve behavior that actually violates these standards.
